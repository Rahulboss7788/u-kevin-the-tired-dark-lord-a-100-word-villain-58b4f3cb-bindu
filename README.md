A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The Dark Lord is kept awake by the castle damp, the moat's paperwork, and having to rehearse his evil laugh every morning — yet tonight, at last, his scheme comes together. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm, and the small deflections, never in a plain statement of weariness.
